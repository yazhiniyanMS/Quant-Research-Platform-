Overview

Paarvai AI is a student-safety technology platform focused on improving school transportation safety and communication between schools, students, conductors, and parents.

Problem

School transportation is hard to monitor in real time, which leaves gaps in attendance verification and in communication between conductors, school administration, and parents.

Product

The product is built around student safety and transportation monitoring — attendance verification, parent alerts, a school administration dashboard, and workflows for students and conductors.

Technology

Web and applied-AI technologies — the same toolkit I use across my projects.

Python TypeScript JavaScript HTML/CSS AI
Current Stage

Actively building core product workflows and working toward school pilots. Developed in part within the MIT Venture Innovation Program ecosystem.

What I'm Learning

Designing for people who aren't necessarily technical — school administrators, conductors, and parents — and learning that a safety-critical product has to earn trust before it earns adoption.
